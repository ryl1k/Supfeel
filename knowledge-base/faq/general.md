# General store information

## About Velora

Velora is an online footwear and apparel brand focused on everyday comfort. Core lines: Cloud Runner (cushioned running), Everyday (casual knit), Trail (outdoor), Court (leather court sneaker), and Slip-On.

## Support hours

Supfeel (the AI assistant) is available 24/7. Human specialists are available Monday–Friday, 8 am–8 pm ET. Escalated cases get a reply within **1 business day**.

## Gift cards

Gift cards are available from $25 to $500. They never expire and cannot be redeemed for cash. Lost gift card codes can be re-sent to the purchaser's email.

## Loyalty points

Customers earn 1 point per $1 spent (2× for Velora Club members). 100 points = $5 off. Points are deducted if an order is refunded.
