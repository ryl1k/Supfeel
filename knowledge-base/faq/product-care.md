# Product care

## Cleaning knit sneakers

Velora Cloud Runner and Velora Everyday uppers are knit. Remove the insoles and laces, then hand wash with cold water and mild soap. Air dry away from direct heat. Machine washing on a cold delicate cycle in a laundry bag is acceptable up to 5 times. Never tumble dry, because heat can separate the soles, and this damage is not covered by the warranty.

## Leather care

For Velora Court leather, wipe with a damp cloth and condition monthly. Avoid soaking.

## Apparel

Machine wash cold, inside out, and hang dry to prevent shrinking.
