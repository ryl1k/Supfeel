# Sizing and fit

## General sizing

Velora sneakers run **true to size** in US sizing. Velora Trail and Velora Court models run about **half a size small**, so we recommend sizing up by half a size. Half sizes are available from 6 to 13.

## Width options

Most models come in **Standard (D)** and **Wide (2E)** widths for men, and Standard (B) and Wide (D) widths for women. Models with wide options: Velora Cloud Runner, Velora Everyday, Velora Trail. The Velora Court and Velora Slip-On come in standard width only.

## Common comfort issues and suggested fixes

| Complaint | Likely cause | Suggestion |
|---|---|---|
| Pinching on the sides or toes | Too narrow | Same model in Wide, or Velora Cloud Runner Wide |
| Heel slipping | Too long / loose | Half size down, or try the heel-lock lacing technique |
| Pressure on top of foot | Low volume | Loosen top eyelets; Velora Everyday has a higher volume |
| Arch pain | Lacking support | Velora Cloud Runner (structured arch) or the Velora Support insole ($24.95) |
| Stiff / hard | Firm midsole | Velora Cloud Runner (softest cushioning) |

## Break-in

Most Velora sneakers need no break-in. Leather models (Velora Court) soften after about one week of wear.
