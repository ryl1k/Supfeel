# Refunds

## Refund methods

Refunds go back to the **original payment method** by default. Customers may choose **Velora store credit** instead, which is issued instantly and includes a **10% bonus** (for example, a $100 refund becomes $110 credit). Store credit does not expire.

## Refund timing

Velora processes approved refunds immediately. Card refunds typically appear in 3–10 business days depending on the bank. PayPal refunds appear within 1–2 business days. Store credit is available immediately.

## Partial refunds and goodwill credits

For minor issues where the customer wants to keep the item (small cosmetic flaw, late delivery, missing accessory), Velora may offer a partial refund of **up to 20% of the item price** or a goodwill store credit of **up to $25**, instead of a return.

## Price adjustment

If an item drops in price within **14 days of purchase**, customers can request a one-time price adjustment for the difference. Price adjustments do not apply to flash sales, clearance or coupon-code discounts.

## Order cancellations

Orders can be cancelled for a full refund while their status is **"processing"** (not yet shipped). Once shipped, the order must be returned instead.

## Refunds for undelivered orders

If a package is confirmed lost (see the shipping policy), the customer can choose either a free reship or a full refund, including original shipping fees.
