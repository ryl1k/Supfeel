# Warranty and defects

## Warranty coverage

All Velora footwear has a **12-month warranty** against manufacturing defects from the delivery date. Covered: sole separation, broken stitching, faulty eyelets or zippers, and material defects under normal use. Not covered: normal wear (sole wear, creasing), damage from misuse, improper cleaning, or accidents.

## Making a warranty claim

The customer provides the order number and a photo of the defect. If the defect is clearly covered, Velora sends a **free replacement** of the same item (or an equivalent if it is out of stock). If no equivalent exists, a full refund is given. For items under $100, the customer does not need to send the defective item back.

## Defects within 30 days

Defects reported within 30 days of delivery are treated like a free return or exchange. The customer chooses replacement, exchange or full refund, and return shipping is free.

## Apparel

Apparel has a **90-day** defect warranty under the same rules.
