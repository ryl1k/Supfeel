# Payments and billing

## Accepted payment methods

Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay and Velora gift cards. Buy-now-pay-later is available through Klarna for orders over $50.

## Authorization holds vs. charges

When an order is placed, the bank may show a **pending authorization hold**. If an order is edited or a payment retry happens, a second hold can appear. Pending holds that were never captured disappear automatically within **3–7 business days** and are not charges. Agents should check whether a payment is `captured` or only `authorized` before treating it as a double charge.

## Duplicate charges

If two **captured** payments exist for the same order and amount, the duplicate is a billing error. Velora refunds the duplicate immediately and in full. No approval is needed, whatever the amount.

## Promo codes

One promo code per order. Codes cannot be applied after an order is placed, except within **24 hours** of placing the order, when the discount can be refunded as a price adjustment. Expired codes cannot be honored.

## Invoices

Invoices are available in the account's order history and can be re-sent by email on request.
