# Account and privacy

## Identity verification

Before making changes to an account or an order, the agent confirms the customer's identity. In the chat this happens through the signed-in session. For guests, the agent confirms the order number **and** the email address on the order. Never reveal full card numbers, full addresses or other customers' data. Show at most the last 4 card digits.

## Updating account details

Customers can update their name, phone and default address themselves or via support. Email address changes require confirmation sent to **both** the old and the new address.

## Password and login

Velora support never asks for passwords. Customers can reset passwords via "Forgot password" on the login page. Accounts lock for 30 minutes after 5 failed login attempts.

## Data deletion and export

Customers can request a data export or account deletion. Deletion completes within **30 days**. Open orders must be completed or cancelled first. Deletion requests are always handed to a human privacy specialist.

## Marketing preferences

Customers can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time. Transactional emails (order and shipping updates) continue.
