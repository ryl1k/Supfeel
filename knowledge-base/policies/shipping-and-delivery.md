# Shipping and delivery

## Shipping options

| Option | Delivery time | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 3–6 business days | $5.95, free on orders over $75 and for Velora Club members |
| Express | 1–2 business days | $14.95 |

Orders placed before 2 pm local time on business days ship the same day.

## Tracking

A tracking link is emailed when the order ships. Tracking may take up to 24 hours to show the first scan.

## Delayed packages

A package is considered **delayed** when it has had no tracking update for **5 business days**, or is more than 3 business days past the estimated delivery date. Velora contacts the carrier. Express shipping fees are refunded automatically for delayed express orders.

## Lost packages

A package is considered **lost** when it has had no tracking update for **7 business days**, or the carrier marks it lost. Velora then offers the customer the choice of a **free reship by express shipping** or a **full refund**. No need to wait for a carrier investigation.

## Marked delivered but not received

If tracking says delivered but the customer has not received the package, the customer should check around the address and with neighbors and wait 48 hours, since carriers sometimes scan early. After 48 hours, Velora files a carrier claim and reships or refunds once. A second claim on the same account within 12 months requires human review.

## Damaged on arrival

If the package or items arrived damaged, the customer should send a photo. Velora ships a free replacement or refunds in full. No return of the damaged item is needed for items under $50.

## Address changes

The shipping address can be changed while the order status is **"processing"**. After shipping, Velora can request a carrier redirect, but this is not guaranteed.

## Shipping regions

Velora ships to the United States and Canada. Canadian orders may take 2 extra business days. Duties are included in the price.
