# Velora Club membership

## Benefits

Velora Club costs **$9.99 per month** or **$89 per year**. Benefits: free standard shipping, free returns, early access to drops, and 2× loyalty points.

## Pausing

Members may **pause** the membership for **1, 2 or 3 months** at no cost, once per 12 months. Benefits stop during the pause and billing resumes automatically afterwards. When a customer asks to cancel, agents should **offer a pause once**. If the customer still wants to cancel, cancel without further pushback.

## Cancelling

Members can cancel any time. Monthly plans stay active until the end of the current billing period, with no partial refund. Annual plans cancelled within **14 days of renewal** get a full refund of that renewal. After 14 days, annual plans are refunded pro rata for the unused full months.

## Unexpected renewal charges

If a member says they did not intend to renew and the renewal was charged within the last **14 days** with no Club benefits used since then (no free-shipping orders, no free returns), refund the renewal in full and cancel.

## Rejoining

Cancelled members can rejoin any time. Loyalty points are kept for 12 months after cancellation.
