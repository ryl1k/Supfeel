# Support FAQ

## Why was I charged a delivery fee?
Delivery is $3.99 unless the order subtotal (after promo discounts) is $25.00 or more.

## Can I use two promo codes?
No. One promo code per order. `WELCOME5` only works on a customer's first order.

## Can I cancel my order?
Yes, while it is `received` or `confirmed`. When it is `baking` the kitchen has started and it can't be cancelled.
Refunds reach the original payment method in 3–5 business days.

## What are your hours?
We accept orders from 10:00 to 23:00.
